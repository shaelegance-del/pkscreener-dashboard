💡 Tips for Better Results:

  • Entry Price: Optimal price to enter the trade (market price or limit)
  • Target Price: Profit-taking level where you exit with gains
  • Stop Loss: Risk management level to exit if trade goes wrong
  • R:R Ratio: Risk-Reward ratio (Target-Entry)/(Entry-SL) - Min 1:2
  • Intraday: Best used with 1-5 min candles during market hours (9:15 AM - 3:30 PM)
  • Swing: Review with 4hr or daily charts for confirmation
  • BTST: Check previous day close & gap analysis
  • Never risk more than 2% of capital per trade
